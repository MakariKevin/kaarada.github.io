*KaaRada App*

Effective Date: **17th September 2025**

KaaRada respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our alerts mobile app (KaaDa App) handles information.
 
**1. Information We Collect**
*   We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
*   Users are not required to sign up, create accounts, or provide any personal details to use the App.
*   The app however uses third-party services that may collect information that maybe used to identify you as user. Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app

*   [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)
*   [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://www.google.com/analytics/terms/)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)
*   [Firebase Cloud Messaging](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
 
**2. Notifications**
*   The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver alerts and notifications.
*   Users must explicitly opt in by granting notification permissions on their device.
*   Users may opt out at any time by turning off notifications in their device settings.
 
**3. How We Use Information**
*   We only use Firebase to send relevant alerts.
*   No personally identifiable information (PII) is linked to these notifications.
*   No tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising is performed.
 
**4. Data Sharing**
*   We do not share any user information with third parties.
*   Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to sell, rent, or trade.
 
**5. Security**
*   Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, a secure and industry-standard service by Google.
*   As we do not collect personal data, risks to your privacy are minimal.
 
**6. Your Choices**
*   You may control or disable notifications through your device settings.
*   Since no personal data is collected, there are no accounts or data to delete.
 
**7. Children’s Privacy**
*   The App is safe for all ages, as no personal information is collected.
*   If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child has shared information with us, please note that the App does not collect such information.
 
**8. Changes to This Policy**
*   We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
*   Updates will be posted within the App or on our official website.
 
**9. Contact Us**
